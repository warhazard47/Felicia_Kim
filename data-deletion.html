FelKim · Effective date: October 1, 2026

How to request deletion of your data

If you have commented on or messaged the Instagram account @feliciaaa_kim and want the information that FelKim stored about you removed, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to mikiehunt3r@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. In the email, include your Instagram username so we can find your records.
  3. We will delete the comments, messages, conversation history and identifiers linked to your Instagram account that we stored, and confirm by email, within 30 days.

What is deleted

Stored comment and message text, conversation history, and your Instagram ID and username in our records.

What we cannot delete

Your comments and messages still exist on Instagram itself, which is operated by Meta. You can delete your own comments and conversations directly in Instagram, and Meta handles that data under its own policies.
