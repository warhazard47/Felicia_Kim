We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Automated and AI-generated replies

Replies from this account are generated automatically using AI language models. To produce a reply, the text of the comment or message you sent is processed by an AI service provider. Captions for new posts may be generated from the image and a short description. AI-generated replies can be wrong, so please do not rely on them for important decisions, and do not share sensitive personal information in comments or messages.
